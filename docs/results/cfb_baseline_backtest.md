## Protocol

- Model: `dfs.projections.cfb` (season averages adjusted for recent workload and game environment).
- Fit: parameters defaults (from an earlier --fit run) and dispersion / p_zero tables fit on 2025 weeks 3-13 with 2024 as the prior season.
- Test: 2026 weeks 3-7, prior season 2025; out of sample. Each week is projected from games strictly before it (`dfs.data.cfb.before`).
- Games: FBS-vs-FBS only (what DK/FD college slates contain). Lines: ESPN pre-game spread/total from the betting file (closing-type lines; a live Wednesday line is noisier).
- Actuals: target-week stat lines from play-by-play (official box used where the pbp is incomplete) scored with `dfs.scoring`; a projected player with no recorded stat scores 0.
- Pools: **dfs** = per team, the top recent-usage QB1, RB1-2, WR/TE1-4 chosen before kickoff (DNPs count as 0; model `mean` includes p_zero). **role** = players who recorded >= 5 touches+targets (QB >= 10 dropbacks+carries) and were projected; model `mean_played` compared; outcome-selected, so its bias column is optimistic for every predictor.
- Naive baselines: season-to-date points per game played, and last game's points. Their 10-90 intervals use the model's fitted dispersion around the naive mean, so coverage compares like with like.

## Known limitations

- Lines: the ESPN betting file holds pre-game lines only for games already played (closing-type). Live slates have no line in these files, so the environment factor is off unless lines are passed to `CFBProvider(lines=...)`; backtest accuracy with lines is therefore a little better than a Wednesday projection would see.
- Sacks are scored as rushing yards (NCAA official convention, `SACKS_IN_RUSHING`); not confirmed against a DK/FD CFB box score. Special-teams fumbles and kneel-downs logged under a team name are not attributed (see validation: fumbles lost vs the box 2024: -19.7%, 2025: -19.3%, 2026: -19.0%; a few tenths of a point per affected player-game).
- Play-by-play gaps: 2024 incompletion texts often omit the target (target shares from 2024 are partial); team-games whose pbp is visibly incomplete are scored from the official box and excluded from usage shares.
- The season aggregate files are themselves derived from cfbfastR pbp, so agreeing with them checks parsing consistency; the ESPN box comparison is the independent check.
- Recent workload adds little on average this early in the season (the last two games are a large share of a 2-6 game season); its value is concentrated in players whose role grew (see role-change table).
- Coverage of the projection set: players with no recorded play in their team's last two games (returning from injury, debutants) are not projected; first-time starters are invisible to a stats-only baseline.
- Positions come from ESPN rosters (TE kept separate from WR even though DK lists TEs as WR); players without a roster entry get a usage-inferred position computed over their whole season.
